Privacy Policy

How Ardson Maritime collects, uses, transfers, and protects the personal data of seafarers and principals under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27/2022, "UU PDP").

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who controls your data

Ardson Maritime Career Center, Purbalingga, Central Java, Indonesia 53352, is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Our data protection contact is ardsonmaritime@outlook.com.

What we collect

We collect only what is needed to place seafarers and to respond to enquiries.

  • Identity and contact — full name, email address, phone / WhatsApp number, and optionally your address.
  • Professional profile — applied rank, vessel type experience, certification status (BST, tanker CoP, medical, passport, seaman's book).
  • Documents you upload — CV, sea service record, and any certificate you choose to attach. These documents can contain identity-document numbers, dates of birth, and photographs.
  • Principal enquiries — company name, contact email, vessel type, joining date, required ranks, contract duration, flag state.
  • Technical data — the IP address that submitted a form, used only for spam protection and security logging.

We do not ask for, and ask that you do not include in any upload: your national ID (KTP) number, bank account or card numbers, passwords, or medical diagnoses beyond a "fit for sea" certificate. If a principal requires an identity document at a later stage, we will tell you exactly which document, why, and for how long.

Why we collect it

  • To assess your profile against current and future vacancies from our principals.
  • To verify certificates and sea service records before a candidate is proposed.
  • To coordinate visas, flag endorsements, medical appointments, and travel.
  • To respond to enquiries sent through the contact form or newsletter signup.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse of the registration form.

Legal basis

Processing is based on your consent, given when you tick the consent box and submit the registration form, and on the performance of the recruitment service you request. Consent is specific to crew placement; we will ask again before using your data for any new purpose. You may withdraw consent at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Who can see your data

Your profile is shared only with the principals whose vacancies you are proposed for, and only the fields required for that proposal. Typical fields are your name, rank, vessel experience, certification status, and CV. Principals are contractually required to use the data only for crew selection and to delete it if you are not selected.

We do not sell personal data, do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and do not run third-party advertising trackers on this site.

International transfers

Because our principals operate internationally, your data may be transferred to a principal outside Indonesia when we propose you for a vacancy. We disclose the receiving principal before the transfer happens, and we require the principal to protect your data at a standard no lower than UU PDP. If you prefer not to have your data transferred to a particular country, tell us and we will not propose you for vacancies there.

How we protect it

  • Uploaded CVs are stored outside the public web root and are accessible only to authorised crewing staff through authenticated access. They are never served by a public URL.
  • Admin access to applicant data is password protected and every CV download is written to the audit log.
  • Passwords, if any are introduced, are stored hashed; form submissions are rate limited; the API sends strict security headers.
  • Only the crewing staff who need your data to do their job have access to it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by UU PDP.

Retention

Seafarer profiles are kept for twenty-four months from the last update, after which they are removed unless you ask us to keep them current. Uploaded CVs follow the same retention period. Contact messages and newsletter records are kept for twelve months. Audit logs of admin actions are kept for twelve months.

Your rights

Under UU PDP you may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you, and a copy of it.
  • Request correction of data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Request deletion of your data and withdrawal of your consent.
  • Object to processing, or ask us to restrict it.
  • Ask us to port your data to another controller in a machine-readable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Indonesian data protection authority.

Requests are answered within fourteen working days. Write to ardsonmaritime@outlook.com with the subject line "Data request" and the email address you registered with.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The admin console uses a single session token in browser storage so that you stay signed in; that token is not used for tracking.

Children

The service is intended for adults who hold or are obtaining STCW certification. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above. Material changes affecting existing applicants will be notified by email.
