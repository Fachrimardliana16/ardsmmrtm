For seafarers

Register and apply in four steps

Registration is free. You are never asked to pay a recruiter, a broker, or a middleman.

Prepare your documents

Have your CoC, STCW endorsements, seaman's book, passport, and medical certificate scanned. A CV in PDF is optional but speeds up matching.

Open the registration form

Use Seafarer Portal in the header, or the Register as crew button on the seafarer page. The form takes about five minutes.

Upload and submit

Attach a PDF, DOC, or DOCX file up to 5MB. You receive a reference number such as AM-2026-01006 — keep it for any follow-up.

Wait for verification

Our crewing desk verifies your documents within two working days, then proposes you only to vacancies that match your rank and vessel experience.

Common questions

Do seafarers pay a recruitment fee?

No. Placement is paid by the principal. Under MLC 2006, no manning agency may charge a seafarer for recruitment or placement, directly or indirectly.

How long does verification take?

Two working days for a complete document set. Missing certificates are the most common reason it takes longer.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, DOC, or DOCX up to 5MB. Scanned images should be converted to PDF before upload.

Can I apply for more than one vacancy?

Yes. One profile covers every application. Applying for a vacancy updates your record but does not close other applications.

What happens if a document expires during a contract?

Our mobilisation desk tracks expiry dates and arranges renewal at the nearest approved centre before the certificate lapses.

How do I correct a mistake in my registration?

Email the crewing desk with your reference number. Do not register a second profile — duplicates slow down verification.
