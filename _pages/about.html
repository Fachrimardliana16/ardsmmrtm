Our mandate

Recruitment without shortcuts

Ardson Maritime Career Center brings together qualified Indonesian seafarers and trusted maritime principals through professional recruitment, verified documentation, and standards aligned with STCW.

Built on safety, competence, and transparent communication, our mission is to support sustainable sea careers while giving ship managers crew they can rely on. Every placement is traceable from the first document check to the joining report.

How we work

The principles behind every placement

Verify first

No candidate is proposed until every certificate and sea service record has been checked against the issuing authority.

No fee to seafarers

Recruitment is paid by the principal. Any request for payment from a seafarer is a red flag and reported.

Transparent contracts

Wages, contract length, and repatriation terms are stated in writing under MLC 2006 before joining.

Stay reachable

A duty officer answers during mobilisation and for the first days on board, at any hour.
