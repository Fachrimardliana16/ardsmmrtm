01 — Safety & STCW

Safety readiness and STCW refreshers

STCW 2010 requires specific refresher training every five years. We help you plan the sequence so a course never expires mid-contract.

Basic Safety Training (BST)

Personal survival techniques, fire prevention and fire fighting, elementary first aid, and personal safety and social responsibility.

Advanced fire fighting

For officers and ratings assigned to fire-fighting duties, with practical drills and breathing apparatus work.

Survival craft & rescue boats

SCRB and fast rescue boat courses, including the proficiency in survival craft requirement for deck officers.

Medical first aid & care

MEFA and medical care aboard ship for the designated medical officer role on vessels without a doctor.

02 — Tanker & special certificates

Tanker endorsements and specialised tonnage

Basic and advanced training for oil, chemical, and liquefied gas tankers, aligned with the flag states most of our principals fly.

Oil tanker familiarisation

Basic training for all crew assigned to oil tanker duties, including hazard awareness and cargo safety.

Advanced oil & chemical

Advanced training for masters, chief engineers, and officers with immediate responsibility for cargo operations.

Liquefied gas tanker

Basic and advanced gas tanker certificates, required for LNG and LPG tonnage and increasingly for dual-fuel vessels.

IGF & polar code

International Code of Safety for Ships using Gases or other Low-flashpoint Fuels, plus polar water operational training where the trade requires it.

02b — Seaman's book

Seaman's book issuance and renewal

The Seaman's Book (Buku Pelaut) is the identity document every Indonesian seafarer signs on with. Expired or mismatched books are the single most common cause of joining delays — we track and renew them before a contract is signed.

New issuance

First-time applications at the local port authority (KSOP), including identity verification, medical clearance, and photo capture. Typical lead time is 5–10 working days.

Renewal before expiry

Books are valid for a maximum of two years. We file renewals 60 days before expiry and hold a scanned copy on file, so a contract signed late in the cycle never waits on paperwork.

Name and rank corrections

Discrepancies between the book, CoC, and passport block embarkation. We coordinate corrections with the issuing port and re-sync records in the mariner profile.

Digital copy in profile

Upload a clear scan of the photo page and validity page to your mariner profile. The crewing team checks the expiry date against your target joining date before proposing you to a principal.
