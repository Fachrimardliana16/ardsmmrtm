Before you write

Answers to the questions we get most

Do you charge seafarers a fee?

No. Placement is paid by the principal. Under MLC 2006, no manning agency may charge a seafarer for recruitment or placement.

How long does registration take?

The form takes about five minutes. Document verification is usually completed within two working days.

Which ranks do you place?

Deck, engine, and hotel ranks from ratings to master and chief engineer, across container, tanker, gas, bulk, offshore, and cruise tonnage.

How fast can a crew request be answered?

A first shortlist is typically returned within one working day of receiving a complete specification.
