Emergency replacement crew

A crew member can't join on time — illness, injury, immigration, or a personal emergency. Send us the gap and we mobilise a verified replacement from our standing pool, typically inside 72 hours.

Submit an emergency request Call the duty line
01 — How it works

Four steps from gap to gangway

A 24/7 duty desk receives the request, pulls a verified stand-by from the pool, and handles documentation and travel in parallel so the joining date doesn't slip.

1 · Request received

The duty desk acknowledges inside 30 minutes and confirms the rank, vessel, port, and target joining.

2 · Candidate proposed

Within 6 hours we send 1–3 verified stand-bys with valid Seaman's Book, CoC, medical, and visas already checked.

3 · Travel booked

Flights, hotel, and shore transfer booked same day. Flag-state and port-state notifications prepared.

4 · Handover complete

Relief crew signs on at the agreed port; handover brief with outgoing officer documented for the master.
