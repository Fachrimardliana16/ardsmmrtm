MLC Compliance

How the Maritime Labour Convention, 2006 is applied to every placement made by Ardson Maritime.

No recruitment fee

Under Regulation 1.4 of MLC 2006, no fee or other charge for recruitment or placement may be borne by the seafarer. Ardson Maritime is paid by the principal. Any demand for payment from a seafarer is a breach of this policy and should be reported to us immediately.

Seafarer employment agreements

Every placement results in a written seafarer employment agreement, in English, signed before joining. It states the wage, contract duration, leave entitlement, repatriation arrangements, and the collective bargaining agreement where one applies.

Hours of work and rest

We place crew only on vessels operating a documented hours-of-work or hours-of-rest regime consistent with MLC 2006 and STCW 2010 fatigue requirements.

Medical fitness

Candidates hold a valid medical fitness certificate issued by a recognised clinic. Where the flag state requires a specific form, that form is used.

Repatriation and welfare

Repatriation costs are the responsibility of the shipowner and are stated in the agreement. Crew have access to the vessel's welfare arrangements and to our duty officer during mobilisation.

Complaints

Seafarers may raise a complaint directly with our crewing desk or with the flag state authority. Complaints are logged and answered in writing. We do not retaliate against crew who raise a complaint in good faith.

Audit

Records for each placement — document verification, contract, joining report — are retained and can be produced for audit by a principal or a flag state inspector.
