Engagement model

How a crew request runs

From your specification to joining, typically under 30 days for ratings and 45 days for senior officers.

Send the specification

Ranks, vessel type, flag, joining port, and contract terms. A short call confirms the standing orders and interview format.

Candidate proposal

Within 24 hours we return a shortlist with CVs, certificate status, sea service, and Marlins scores.

Interview and selection

Interviews arranged around your time zone. We coordinate technical assessments and reference checks.

Mobilisation

Contract, visa, flag endorsement, medical, and travel handled end to end, with a joining report before departure.
