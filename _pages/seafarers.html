What you get

Support at every stage of the contract

From the first document check to the flight home, one coordinator stays with your file.

Document verification

We check your CoC, STCW endorsements, seaman's book, passport, and medical against the principal's requirements before proposing you.

Direct matching

Your profile is proposed only to vacancies that match your rank, vessel experience, and joining window. No blanket applications.

MLC 2006 protection

Written employment agreements, transparent wages, and no agency fee. If a contract does not meet MLC 2006, we do not place it.

Mobilisation

Visa, flag state endorsement, medical scheduling, and air ticket are coordinated by our mobilisation desk.

Career progression

Guidance on moving from bulk to tanker or LNG, upgrading your CoC, and building a record principals want to re-hire.

24/7 support

A duty officer is reachable during mobilisation and for the first days on board.

The process

How registration works

Most placements complete within four to six weeks of a complete profile.

Register

Complete the registration form with your rank, vessel experience, and certificate status. Upload a CV or sea service record.

Verification

Our crewing team reviews your documents and confirms which flag endorsements you still need.

Interview

A technical interview with the principal's superintendent, plus a Marlins or equivalent English test.

Joining

Contract signed under MLC 2006, visa and endorsement processed, ticket issued, joining confirmed in writing.

Document checklist

What to prepare before you register

Have these scanned and ready. Missing documents are the most common reason a joining date slips.

Certificate of CompetencyValid for the rank, with STCW 2010 endorsements.
Seaman's bookAt least six blank pages, valid beyond the contract end date.
PassportValid for at least twelve months beyond the joining date.
Medical fitnessMCU or equivalent from a clinic recognised by the flag state.
Basic Safety TrainingBST refreshed within the past five years.
Sea service recordsDischarge book or letters from each principal.
